## Conservative implementation decisions

- The brief's $65 small-claims shorthand is not the fee/track for a local property appeal. Use formal/informal ATB local-property procedures and official assessed-value fee schedule.
- No actual-bill date is inferred from fiscal year. Historical analysis and current deadlines remain distinct.
- Local Saturday/Sunday/holiday endings move to the next standard business day under §59, whose text also covers authorized municipal closures. Suffolk observances show an office-closure warning and safer target; the application has no live office calendar. ATB Rule 1.36 is separately cited.
- Deemed denial uses the nominal three-calendar-month checkpoint conservatively, with office-closure/extension disclosure. This can be an earlier planning date; confirm jurisdictional dates with the authority.
- Late decisions do not silently restart time. Written extensions stop inferred dates. Chronology errors withhold ATB dates.
- Holiday logic cannot know emergency closures/newly declared holidays.

Massachusetts legal review remains appropriate before broader public production use. Level provides legal information and explicit uncertainty, not individualized filing advice.
